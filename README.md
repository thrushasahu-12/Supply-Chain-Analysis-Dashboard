# 🚚 Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a supply chain dataset to identify delivery delays, sales performance, profit trends, and shipping efficiency. The dashboard was developed using **SQL Server** for data analysis and **Power BI** for interactive visualization.

The goal of this project is to help business stakeholders understand operational performance and make better decisions based on data.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

A company is experiencing frequent customer complaints about delayed deliveries.

Business leaders want answers to questions such as:

- Which countries have the highest number of delayed deliveries?
- Which shipping method performs the best?
- Are deliveries taking longer than the promised delivery date?
- Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
- How do delivery delays change over time?

Instead of manually checking thousands of records, this dashboard provides all the answers in one place.

# 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is divided into three sections.

## 1️⃣ Executive KPIs

These cards provide a quick summary of business performance.

| KPI | Meaning |
|------|----------|
| Total Orders | Total number of customer orders processed |
| Total Sales | Total revenue generated from all orders |
| Total Profit | Total profit earned from completed orders |
| Late Delivery % | Percentage of orders delivered later than promised |
| Average Scheduled Days | Average number of days promised to customers for delivery |
| Average Actual Shipping Days | Average number of days taken to deliver orders |

These KPIs allow managers to understand the overall health of the business in a few seconds.

---

## 2️⃣ Delivery Performance

### Late Orders by Country

**Business Question**

> Which countries experience the highest number of late deliveries?

**Why it matters**

Countries with consistently high delivery delays may require improvements in logistics, warehouse operations, or transportation.

**Example Insight**

The dashboard helps identify countries where delivery performance needs immediate attention.

---

### Delivery Performance by Shipping Mode

**Business Question**

> Which shipping method experiences the most delays?

**Why it matters**

Different shipping methods have different delivery speeds.

By comparing shipping modes, managers can identify which service requires operational improvement.

---

### Monthly Late Delivery Trend

**Business Question**

> How do delivery delays change throughout the year?

**Why it matters**

This chart helps identify seasonal increases in delivery delays.

Managers can prepare additional resources during high-risk months.

# 🎛 Dashboard Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters.

| Filter | Purpose |
|---------|----------|
| Region | Analyze a specific region |
| Country | Analyze a specific country |

When a filter is selected, every KPI and chart updates automatically.

This allows users to perform detailed analysis without writing SQL queries.
